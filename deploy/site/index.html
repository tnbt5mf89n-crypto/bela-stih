Ukratko: poslužitelj ne pohranjuje vaše podatke

  • Igra ne traži račun, prijavu ni e-mail. Vaš napredak (razina, novčići, statistika) i povijest partija s prijateljima (njihovi nadimci i rezultati) spremaju se samo na vašem uređaju.
  • Kod online igre poslužitelju se šalju samo: nadimak koji sami upišete, odabrani avatar, vaši potezi u igri te oznaka emotikona ili dara koji pošaljete (npr. „smile”, „kava”) — obična riječ s popisa, nikad vlastiti tekst — te glasovne poruke koje sami snimite (vidi sljedeću točku). Uz to aplikacija šalje nasumičnu oznaku instalacije (broj koji sama stvori jednom, bez veze s vama ili uređajem) i oznake igrača koje ste blokirali: služe samo tome da blokirani igrači ne sjedaju za vaš stol i da prijava može reći o kome je. Ti se podaci obrađuju samo u memoriji dok traje partija i nigdje se trajno ne spremaju.
  • Glasovne poruke: mikrofon se koristi samo dok držite tipku s mikrofonom ili, ako ste tako odabrali u postavkama, između dva dodira na nju (najviše 15 sekundi). Snimka ide poslužitelju, koji je odmah proslijedi ostalim igračima za tim stolom i ne sprema je; njihovi je uređaji samo reproduciraju i brišu, a vašem javljaju kad je poruka poslušana. Uz snimku ide i njezina prosječna glasnoća (broj), da bi je slušatelj čuo jednako glasno kao ostale. Za stolom s nepoznatima njihove poruke čujete tek kad ih za taj stol uključite. Glasovne poruke možete isključiti u postavkama, a pojedinog igrača utišati, sakriti ili trajno blokirati.
  • Kao i kod svake internetske veze, poslužitelj tijekom partije vidi vašu IP adresu; ne bilježimo je i ne koristimo ni za što osim za samu vezu. Tehnički zapisnici poslužitelja automatski se brišu rotacijom.
  • Nema oglasa, nema praćenja, nema analitike trećih strana, nema prodaje podataka.
  • Novčići u igri su virtualni: zarađuju se isključivo igranjem, ne mogu se kupiti ni zamijeniti za pravi novac.
  • Igrača za stolom možete sakriti (samo na vašem uređaju, ništa se ne šalje) ili prijaviti. Prijava je e-poruka koju šaljete vi, iz svoje aplikacije za poštu; poslužitelj igre je ne prima.

In short: the server does not store your data

  • No account, no sign-in, no e-mail. Your progress (level, coins, statistics) and the history of your matches with friends (their nicknames and the scores) are stored on your device only.
  • For online play the server receives only: the nickname you type in, your chosen avatar, your moves and the id of any emote or gift you send (e.g. "smile", "kava") — a word from a fixed list, never text of your own — and any voice message you record (see the next point). The app also sends a random installation ID (a number it makes once, unrelated to you or your device) and the IDs of players you have blocked: they only keep blocked players away from your table and let a report say whom it is about. That data is processed in memory for the duration of the match and is never stored.
  • Voice messages: the microphone is used only while you hold the microphone button or, if you chose so in Settings, between your two taps on it (15 seconds at most). The recording goes to the server, which passes it straight on to the other players at that table and does not store it; their devices only play it, delete it, and tell yours when it has been heard. Each recording carries its average loudness (a number) so listeners hear it as loud as the others. At a table with strangers you hear their messages only once you turn them on for that table. You can switch voice messages off in Settings, and mute, hide or block a single player.
  • As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address during a match; we do not log it or use it for anything beyond the connection itself. Technical server logs are rotated away automatically.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no selling of data.
  • In-game coins are virtual: earned by playing only, and can never be bought or exchanged for real money.
  • You can hide a player at your table (on your device only; nothing is sent) or report them. A report is an e-mail you send yourself, from your own mail app; the game server never receives it.

Rules of conduct

Other players at the table see your nickname; emotes and gifts are for fun. By entering a nickname you accept these rules:

  • No insults, threats, hate speech or discrimination, sexual content, impersonation or other people's personal data in a nickname.
  • Do not use emotes, gifts or voice messages to insult, threaten or harass others.
  • Child safety: any sexual content involving minors, and any grooming, sexualised contact or solicitation of minors through the game, is forbidden. Such reports (prijave@belastih.com) are handled first and passed to the authorities.
  • You can mute, hide or report a player: tap their seat at the table → Report.
  • We review reports within 7 days. An objectionable nickname is blocked on the server and shown as "Player N" instead.

Player reports

When you report a player, we receive the e-mail you send: the reported nickname, the table code, the time, the app version, your e-mail address and whatever you write (voice messages are never stored, so a report cannot include one — describe what was said). We use it to handle the report and to protect players from abuse (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is kept in the mailbox of the reports address, with that address's e-mail provider, and deleted at most 90 days after the report is handled. The game server never receives it.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Apart from player reports (see above) nothing is stored, so there is usually nothing to hand over or erase — but do get in touch. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority; in Croatia that is AZOP (azop.hr).

Deleting your data

You delete everything yourself: choose “Erase progress” in Settings, or simply uninstall the app. There is nothing on the server to delete.
